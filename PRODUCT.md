# PRODUCT — Supercompute

> Product truth. Captured 2026-08-22 from operator direction (Orami). Visual decisions live in DESIGN.md / tokens; this file is the *why*.

## What

Supercompute is an **AI operator for Web3**. A fleet of autonomous agents on Base Chain handles the complexity of crypto — research, trading, publishing, education, infrastructure — so everyday users get the benefit without the technical burden.

## Why (the core message)

Crypto's power is locked behind complexity: wallets, gas, protocols, governance, tokenomics. AI is the simplification layer — agents translate, execute, and guard on the user's behalf. Supercompute exists to **reduce the technical debt of crypto for everyday users**. That is the whole reason the operator path and Hermes exist: one operator, AI in the loop, humans liberated from the machinery.

**The bridge in one line:** *Crypto is complex. Your AI operator makes it simple.*

## Audience

- **Everyday users first** — the curious and the capable: people who want Web3's benefits without becoming protocol engineers.
- **Builders second** — the fleet, the school's advanced tracks, TradeDesk operators.
- The operator-terminal aesthetic stays as texture and identity; the *language* speaks plainly to the first audience.

## Values

Sovereignty, transparency, regeneration, community. Liberation technology — not extraction machinery.

## Mode

Homepage is a Persuade surface (landing) but must read as Operate-friendly for non-technical visitors: one clear action, plain claims, no jargon walls.

## Facts that must stay truthful

- One operator, hands-on since 2013, on Base Chain.
- 13 agents in the fleet; 14 projects shipped.
- Curriculum coverage numbers are build-state (how much of each path is built and live), not user progress.
