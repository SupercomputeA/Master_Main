§01 · Character Specs

Two classes, one people

ORONATION is the SolarPunk Black character system. It defines a single lineage of space-faring Black people rendered in two class palettes: SOLAR (Black & Gold) and SOLAR·BIANCO (Black & White & Gold). Subjects span the full lifespan — babe, youth, adult, elder — across male, female, and curvy/muscular body types. Garments are tailored and ceremonial, never denim, casual, or streetwear; the accent language is jewelry only. There are no symbols, logos, glyphs, or faction marks anywhere on the figure.

Key Style Features

Sacred iconography vocabulary

Hair
White / silver — chosen-lineage trait. Voluminous afro, sculpted locs, or braided styles. The hair is ceremonial regalia.
Eyes
Large, expressive, anime-influenced. Gold-leaf or icy blue irises only — no other hues. Direct, calm gaze.
Collar
High mandarin collar. Deep V-neck on female figures. Structured shoulder silhouette — never shoulder pads.
Halo
Circular gold ring motif behind the head. Byzantine sacred iconography — THE symbol, not a symbol. Rotating radial halo: ancestral family glyph passed to the child.
Jewelry
Layered draped gold chains (vestments), large hoop earrings, thin gold circlets, cuffs, torques. The ONLY accent language permitted.
Rendering
Semi-realistic digital painting. Anime-influenced face on realistic anatomy. Soft luminous lighting. No cartoon, no streetwear, no armor.
Guild Classes
Spacer (space suit) · Earthside (everyday) · Ascendant (full ethereal self) · Space Ship (vessel). Jewelry is the costume across all.
Wrist Tech
Wearable wrist tech — technology on the wrist, not armor. Astro-Quantum Compass, star-chart interface. Same jewelry aesthetic raised to instrument-grade.

§05 · Modes · Space Bound · Earth Bound

Two settings, one character

Space Bound Astral · Ascendant

Kai-Ellion Thomas. Astro Navigator of the Ascended — celestial cartographers exploring cosmic consciousness and protecting ancient knowledge. White ceremonial coat filigreed in 24k gold. Open chest plate, mandarin collar, layered gold chain. Gravity Tuner dagger, Astro-Quantum Compass on wrist. The stars do not guide the lost. They remind us who we are.

Earth Bound Terrestrial · Courtly

White draped jackets and robes on a dark cast base. Heavy gold chains, medallions, earrings, headpieces. Calm, ceremonial posture; no symbols or faction language. This is the Earth-bound register — the same lineage at home, adorned in ceremony.

§08 · Symbolism · Gold · White · Circle · Line

The language has four marks

○
Gold
Divinity, lineage, wealth — the sole accent permitted. Worn, not painted.
□
White / Ivory
Purity, ceremony, chosen-lineage hair. Never blue-white.
◯
Circle
Halo rings, hoop earrings, circlets — no end, no start. The orb of the wearer.
Line
Gold rule hairline dividers, filigree, chain links. The structural constant.
Adorned — Not Armored
SOLAR — where jewelry is costume, and costume is devotion.
